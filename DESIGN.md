# Foam Ledger design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want a clear path from vehicle condition to scheduled care. The page should reduce uncertainty and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Flight deck — `#102332`
- Instrument blue — `#2A6F97`
- Sky wash — `#DDECF2`
- Cloud — `#F7F9F8`
- Approach lime — `#A9D45A`
- Marker orange — `#E78A43`
- Display: Barlow Condensed with Arial Narrow fallback
- Body: Manrope with Trebuchet fallback

## Layout thesis

Reuse the route frame as a checklist card: assess condition, choose scope, confirm the visit, and review the finish.

## Signature

A restrained instrument track marks only the information the driver needs to move from condition to appointment.

## Critique

The first direction risked airplane clichés. The revision removes wings and aviation photography; the pilot idea survives only as functional checklist and instrument geometry.
